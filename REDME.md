Project Title: Tracking violence in Hip-Hop

Project Description: I have collected lyrics from 5 of the most popular rap songs from each decade from the past 40 years. So I collected data from 5 songs from the 1980s, 5 from the 1990s and so on up until 2026.
                     I am conducting text analysis on the lyrical data I have collected from these songs, where I am looking for indications of physical violence being mentioned in the songs and comparing the 
                     frequency of mentions of physical violence to the other decades. My goal is to gauge whether or not the lyrical content of rap music has gotten more violent over the years and point to real 
                     world events that could have influenced this sentiment of violence

Rationale Statement: My motivations behind this project come from many conversations with elder black people who constantly lament that new music from younger generations is much more violent and how that can't
                     be good for society. However, this attitude has always been interesting to me because crime rates have decreased from decade to decade and I believe that art imitates life and if music has 
                     gotten more violent but crime rates have dropped decade after decade then what could be the cause of these new expressions of violence.

Workflow: The libraries I will be using are Lyricsgenius, pandas, nltk along with the genius.com api. I am using the Lyricsgenius library in coordination with the genius api to webscrape the web pages for certain
          rap songs. I am then appending that data into a specific list based on what decade that song is from. After collecting my data, I am using nltk to tokenize the data and and analyze the text within those
          lists.
